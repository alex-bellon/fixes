** view all users with root permissions
#+BEGIN_SRC
getent passwd | cut -d : -f 1 | xargs groups
#+END_SRC

[[https://serverfault.com/questions/208347/how-do-i-list-all-superusers][source]]

** ignore lid shutting (don't turn off screen when laptop is closed)

#+BEGIN_SRC
sudo vim /etc/systemd/logind.conf
#+END_SRC

and uncomment the line

#+BEGIN_SRC
HandleLidSwitch=ignore
#+END_SRC

Log out and log in to apply changes. Re-comment the line to enable it again.
